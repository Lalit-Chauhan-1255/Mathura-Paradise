ABOUT MATHURA

The Sacred City of Mathura

Mathura is one of the most ancient and culturally significant cities of India. Situated on the banks of the Yamuna River, the city is widely known for its deep connection with Lord Krishna and the traditions of Braj.

Mathura is surrounded by a rich spiritual landscape of temples, ghats, historic sites and traditional settlements. Together with Vrindavan and other destinations of Braj, it forms an important centre of pilgrimage, culture and tourism.

The city attracts visitors throughout the year who come to experience its temples, festivals, devotional traditions, local culture, architecture and peaceful atmosphere along the Yamuna.

A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

History of Mathura

A city where ancient history, spirituality and living traditions come together.

01

Ancient Mathura

Mathura has a long and remarkable history and has been an important centre of culture, religion and trade for centuries. Its location along the Yamuna helped it develop into an important settlement in northern India.

02

Krishna Tradition

Mathura holds a central place in the traditions associated with Lord Krishna. According to Hindu tradition, Krishna was born in Mathura, making the city an important pilgrimage destination for devotees.

03

Cultural Heritage

Over the centuries, Mathura developed a distinctive artistic and cultural identity. The region became known for its sculpture, religious traditions, architecture and connections with several major Indian cultures.

04

Mathura Today

Today, Mathura continues to preserve its ancient traditions while welcoming visitors from across India and around the world. Its temples, ghats, festivals, markets and nearby Vrindavan make it a major destination in the Braj region.
